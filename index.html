📄 Abstract
Kowé · ENSBBA-UNSTIM · Abomey Plateau, Benin · July 2025
English
Français
Globalization has led to the abandonment of traditional dietary habits in favour of Western diets, causing food insecurity, malnutrition and the resurgence of chronic diseases. This study evaluates the production techniques and nutritional quality of Kowé, a traditional cowpea-based food from the Abomey Plateau. A technological survey was conducted among 119 producers in six municipalities (Abomey, Agbangnizoun, Bohicon, Djidja, Za-Kpota and Zogbodomey). Main ingredients are white cowpea (98%), corn, palm oil, salt, pepper and potash. Nutritionally, Kowé contains 64.02 g/100g water, 23.95 g/100g lipids, 18.38 g/100g fibre, 34.36 g/100g carbohydrates and 14.28 g/100g protein. Kowé is a food of major cultural and nutritional interest.

Keywords: Kowé · Traditional foods · Production systems · Malnutrition · Public health
La mondialisation a entraîné un abandon des habitudes alimentaires traditionnelles au profit de régimes occidentaux, provoquant insécurité alimentaire, malnutrition et recrudescence des maladies chroniques. Cette étude évalue les techniques de production et la qualité nutritionnelle du Kowé, aliment traditionnel à base de niébé du Plateau d'Abomey. Une enquête a été menée auprès de 119 productrices dans six communes. Le Kowé contient 64,02 g/100g d'eau, 23,95 g/100g de lipides, 18,38 g/100g de fibres, 34,36 g/100g de glucides et 14,28 g/100g de protéines. C'est un aliment d'intérêt culturel et nutritionnel majeur.

💫 Motivation
What drives me every day

🌍 Born in the same context as the populations I serve

I was born and raised in Lissazounmè, a rural village in Benin. I have seen firsthand what malnutrition means in a family, in a community. For me, this is not a statistic — it is a reality I lived before I studied it.

This closeness to the field gives me the drive to work with rigour, even when resources are scarce. I know why I work. And for whom.

🔬 Science in service of the field

Research only makes sense if it changes something in people's lives. My goal is to produce data that enables community health workers to detect malnutrition earlier and act faster.

🎯 Excellence through demand

Top of class every year, 19/20 on my thesis, 7 international certifications — not competition. A standard I set because the problem I want to solve deserves the best possible preparation.

🚀 Why an international opportunity?

Because malnutrition in Benin deserves world-class methods. Advanced training will give me the analytical tools, scientific network and credibility to build a lasting surveillance system for future generations.
